## Recorded instruments used in composed audio

Project production manifests identify recorded instruments from
[VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE), under
[CC0-1.0](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE/blob/master/LICENSE).
The library is by Versilian Studios; recordings are credited to Sam Gossner and
Simon Dalzell, with sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote.

These instrument recordings are used in composed game cues, including
`Assets/Resources/Audio/SFX_WinScreen.ogg` and
`Assets/Resources/Audio/SFX_WinCardReveal.wav`. Raw instrument libraries and
intermediate composition files are not included in this repository.

## Glass-clink recording

[“Two Small Wine Glasses Clinking Sound” by olehenriksen](https://freesound.org/people/olehenriksen/sounds/771254/)
is recorded in the project manifests as CC0-1.0. An edited portion is used in the
game's composed audio, including `Assets/Resources/Audio/SFX_WinScreen.ogg` and
`Assets/Resources/Audio/SFX_StartupLogo.ogg`.
